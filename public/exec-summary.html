The problem

F&B chains that run kiosks inside hypermarkets don't own the POS. The official sales report arrives 3–4 weeks late; card settlements land in batches net of MDR; SST and LHDN MyInvois compliance can't wait for spreadsheets. Operations, finance and tax need one system that estimates revenue in real time and reconciles the truth when it eventually arrives.

What it does

📷Real-time QR Sales
Phone-camera scan (iOS + Android), automatic stock deduction, recipe-aware consumption, offline-safe queue.
📦QR Stock Control
Receive, transfer, consume, wastage, count — every movement traceable from supplier to sale, FEFO-aware.
🍳Central Kitchen Production
Recipe / BOM production orders with moving-average cost on the produced output.
🚚Procurement → AP
PO → approve → GRN with batch/expiry → auto-raised supplier invoice (NET 30). CSV bulk PO import.
📊Multi-outlet & AI Dashboards
Worker / outlet / HQ / AI dashboards · forecasting · low-stock + expiry alerts · purchase recommendations.
🔁Dual Reconciliation
QR sales vs delayed hypermarket report · card-terminal vs bank statement with MDR-fee tolerance.
💰Operational Finance
Accounts Payable + Accounts Receivable, P&L (revenue / COGS / gross profit), inventory valuation.
🧾SST + LHDN e-Invoice
Service Tax 6 % split on every sale; per-sale (B2B) and consolidated B2C e-invoice with validation QR.
↩️Sales Corrections
Discounts, voids and refunds with stock reversal; flow into P&L and SST; manager-only (fraud control).
🔒Period Close
Closed accounting periods reject backdated activity; reopen if a fix is genuinely needed.
👥Roles & RBAC
Super-admin, HQ, outlet & restaurant manager, accountant, worker — every action audit-logged.
⬆️Bulk Import
Catalogue, opening stock, and supplier POs via CSV — onboard fast in the initial stage.

Daily operating flow

Buy→ Produce (CK)→ Distribute→ QR Sell→ Reconcile (sales + bank)→ Account (AP/AR/P&L)→ e-Invoice→ Close period

Why it matters

  • Replaces POS dependency — sales captured by QR scan in real time, no hypermarket till required.
  • Malaysian-native — SST 6 % + LHDN MyInvois UBL JSON out of the box, ready for live submission.
  • Built for kiosk reality — works offline (queue + idempotent sync), iOS & Android camera scan via jsQR.
  • Self-hosted on Hostinger — pure PHP 8 + MySQL 8, zero external runtime dependencies, low operating cost.
  • Tamper-evident — full audit log on every change, period close protects monthly figures.
